Value isn't something a thing has by itself. It shows up only when products meet each other in exchange, and it gets its independent form only in money. That's the thread this blog pulls on: the critique of the value-form read through Heinrich, and what it would mean to abolish that form outright instead of managing it.

> The mysterious character of the commodity-form consists therefore simply in the fact that the commodity reflects the social characteristics of men's own labour as objective characteristics of the products of labour themselves.[^1]
